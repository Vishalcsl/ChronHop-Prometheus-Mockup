Our approach

How we would correct the Artificial Intelligence (AI) chat assistant

The assistant was not broken because it was unkind. It was broken because it was confident without facts. So we fix what it knows, what it may say, and what happens when it does not know.

1
Find out what really went wrongRead the chats from that morning. Sort every wrong answer into three piles: it guessed a fact, it invented a policy, or it should have handed over. This tells us which fix matters most.
2
Connect it to live factsGive it read-only access to the Seat Book, the customer's own booking and the price list. If the fact is not there, the honest answer is "I cannot see that".
3
Write a one-page rulebookWhat it may answer alone, what it must hand to a person, and what it must never say. Signed off by support, legal and the founder before any sale.
4
Add a safety check on every replyA second, simple check reads each message before it is sent. If it contains a promise, a price or a refund amount that did not come from the system, the message is replaced with a handover.
5
Make handover fast and humanOne tap to a person, with the chat history attached and a clear wait time. When chat volume passes five times normal, the assistant switches to a short holding message and a queue.
6
Test before every sale, then keep watchingThirty trick questions, including five about things that have never happened. It goes live only with zero promises. After launch, a person reviews a sample of chats every week and support leads have a one-click off switch.

Off limits

  • A price that is not on the price list
  • A policy the company has not published
